# Flex and the Three Doors — Interactive Story (ages 5–8)

Hero: **Flex**, a brave kid in a spider costume. Catchphrase: **"Flex it out!"** (viewers shout it at every choice).
Tone: gentle-spooky, never scary. Every "monster" turns out friendly. Run time: ~2–3 min per episode.

## How the interactivity works (YouTube-native)
- Ep 1 ends on a choice. End screen + cards link to the 3 branch videos (Door 1 / 2 / 3).
- Vote via a **Community post** poll posted before Ep 1 goes live (channel is Made for Kids, so comments are off).
- Every branch ends with a link to the Finale and a "Try another door!" end-screen, so viewers watch all branches (more session time).
- Playlist: "Flex and the Three Doors" ordered Ep 1 → Door 1 → Door 2 → Door 3 → Finale.

```
Ep 1 (clip) ──► Door 1: Knock    ──► friendly ghost  ─┐
              ├► Door 2: Bell     ──► kind witch      ─┼─► Finale: Pumpkin Party
              └► Door 3: Window   ──► dancing pumpkins ┘
```

## Ep 1 — "Flex Finds a Spooky House" (uses your 8s clip as the opener)

**0:00–0:08 (clip, narrator over it):**
NARRATOR: "It's Halloween night! Flex has his spider costume, his candy bucket… and a very big smile!"
FLEX: "Flex it out! Let's get some candy!"

**0:08–0:40 (new clips to generate):**

- FLEX: "Whoa… this house looks SO spooky. But I'm a spider — spiders are brave!"
- Close-up of the door. A tiny glow comes from the keyhole.

**0:40–1:00 — THE CHOICE:**
NARRATOR: "Flex sees three ways to say hello. Which one should he pick?"
- Door 1: **Knock, knock, knock** (door appears)
- Door 2: **Ring the bell** (doorbell appears)
- Door 3: **Peek in the window** (window appears)
FLEX (to camera): "You pick! Tap a door on the screen, or vote in our Community post. Flex it out!"

---

## Door 1 — "Knock, Knock, Knock"
1. Flex knocks three times. The door creaks open… nobody there.
2. A floating white shape: "BOO!" Flex jumps, then giggles — it's **Gus the Ghost**, who is shy and wants a friend.
3. Gus: "Everyone runs away. Nobody stays to play." Flex: "I'm not scared. Want to trick-or-treat together?"
4. Gus gives Flex a glowing **star candy**. Lesson: *being kind beats being scared.*
5. End: "Tap Finale to see what happens next, or try another door!"

## Door 2 — "Ring the Bell"
1. Ding-dong! A friendly **witch, Wilma**, opens with a cat on her shoulder.
2. Wilma: "Oh good, a helper! My broom is stuck in my pumpkin patch." Flex helps pull it out — pop!
3. Wilma gives Flex a **bubbling "giggle potion"** (it's lemon soda). Everyone laughs.
4. Lesson: *helping others feels great.*
5. End: Finale + "try another door".

## Door 3 — "Peek in the Window"
1. Flex peeks in: a room full of **dancing jack-o'-lanterns** in a conga line.
2. A pumpkin waves Flex in: "We lost our dance leader!" Flex does a spider dance.
3. Pumpkins hand over a bucket-full of candy.
4. Lesson: *joining in is fun.*
5. End: Finale + "try another door".

---

## Finale — "The Pumpkin Party"
Gus, Wilma and the pumpkins all meet in the yard for a party. Flex shares candy with everyone.
FLEX: "The best treat isn't candy… it's friends! Happy Halloween! Flex it out!"
Closing prompt: "Which door was YOUR favorite? Vote in our Community post!" + subscribe prompt.

## Virality design (from kids-content patterns; replace with your analytics when linked)
- Hook in first 3 seconds (the clip's face + pumpkin does this).
- A choice every episode = repeat views and branch-hopping.
- A repeated catchphrase kids can shout back.
- Friendly "scary" payoff: parents are comfortable sharing.
- Bright, high-contrast, face-forward visuals for thumbnails.
