# Titles, Descriptions, Hashtags, Schedule

## Made for Kids (recommended setting)
- Set every video to **Made for Kids**. This is required for child-directed content and turns off comments, notifications and personalized ads. Vote through **Community posts** and end screens instead.
- Keep content free of scary imagery beyond the friendly style used here.

## Posting schedule (adjust to your timezone and verify with YouTube Analytics → "When your viewers are on YouTube")
Kids' audience usually peaks after school (≈3–6 pm local) and weekend mornings.

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| T–5 | Community post teaser + Short #1 |
| T–4 | Ep 1 (long, 4 pm) + Short #2 |
| T–3 | Community poll closes → Short #3 |
| T–2 | Door 1 + Short #4 |
| T–1 | Door 2 and Door 3 (same day, 4 pm and 6 pm) |
| Halloween | Finale (morning) + Short #5 |

## Analytics checklist (once you have data)
- Pull top-5 videos by **average view duration** and **click-through rate**; copy their thumbnail style and title length.
- Check which end-screen branch gets the most clicks and promote that door.
- A/B test the 3 thumbnails (YouTube "Test & compare") for 2 weeks.
- Re-run Virale after linking the channel for niche patterns and quick wins.
